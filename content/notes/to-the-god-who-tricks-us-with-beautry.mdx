#### *By Aryan Ansarizadeh · Originally published Oct 7, 2026* ####
-
*Fool I have lied,*  
*The truth for some time.*  
*No one shall know what’s what,*  
*For I shall lie and die!*  

*Some live the line unbroken,*  
*A few are troubled — they sleep.*  
*Soon, the turn will be over, so*  
*If you are waiting, do not wait, just leap.*  

*My God to you, this land of sand,*  
*Gone with a wind you blow.*  
*Such fall can call the sirens to bay,*  
*Fallen I am, and golden I say, yet I do not seem to glow.*  

*To you, God, I am but this fool,*  
*Half lived, half dead, half gone away.*  
*Holy water shall do me good,*  
*But this drought has drowned sin with sin.*  

*Cry me a dune, for rivers run dry.*  
*Sing me a song, you siren of gods.*  
*Tell of the land where I live in full,*  
*Away from wretched filth of rule.*

*Poisoning beauty, you liar of lies!*  
*Hell was just heaven, but not for you.*  
*God of trickery and falsely truth,*  
*I have come to woe, in this life, just you.*

*My life has been ruined, so you could see,*  
*With blinding lights a’twinkle in your eyes.*  
*I wanted to shine as bright as stars,*  
*But you have got me slaved with only scars.*  

*It was you, or perhaps me,*  
*That left me alone leaving.*  
*Cleaning so deep, I find peace in only sleep,*  
*What fate for me now are you weaving?*
