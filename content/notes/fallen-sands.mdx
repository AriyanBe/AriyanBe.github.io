#### *By Aryan Ansarizadeh · Originally published September 20, 2026* ####
-
*Longing the long road — never coming to end.  
Yearned for so many years with nothing earned.  
Searched the most wretched through filth and guilt.  
And at last, found that lasting peace — love.*
*Saw the sowed seed and the fruit of its creed,  
Its promises heard from the wings of its dove.  
I saw with glass eyes its angel fly by.  
She dressed the air fresh with peach and color dare.*
*I touched her loving hair with love and with care.  
She clasped my hands tight, so tight I had grabbed,  
But now I am lone — with hands full of sand.  
My time is now shattered, my heart has torn my peace,*
*And that last piece of love, it’s been lost at last.  
I shall build my castle tall with torments of my past.  
With my heart still longing and these red cut up hands,  
I will build my castle here with these wet fallen sands.*
